The story

Started in 2014. Still here. Still doing it himself.

Danny spent his twenties on other people's crews. He got good at the work — and got a front-row seat to every shortcut the industry runs on. Subcontractors who disappear after the sale. Estimators who quote one thing and install another. Material swaps nobody told the homeowner about.

When he started Peak Edge in 2014, the rule was simple: if he wouldn't put it on his own mother's house, it doesn't go on yours. That's held up through 11 years, 1,200+ roofs, and more hail seasons than he can count.

The crew is small by design — four guys who've worked together for years. No revolving door of subs. Danny is on every roof before the quote, during the install, and after the final walkthrough. If something isn't right, he fixes it before he leaves.

How we work

The rules that don't change. Ever.

Owner on every job

Danny inspects every roof before quoting, visits during the install, and does the final walkthrough. There's no estimator who hands off to a sub you've never met. If you have a question, you call Danny directly.

Show up when you say you will

It sounds obvious. In this industry it's rare. If we schedule an inspection for Tuesday at 9, Danny is there at 9. If something comes up, he calls you — not the other way around.

Tell the homeowner the truth

If the roof doesn't need replacing, Danny will tell you that. He's left jobs where he could have sold a $14,000 replacement and instead told the owner they have three good years left. Those homeowners call back — with their neighbors.

Clean site, every day

Roofing is loud and messy. We clean up our debris every day before we leave. Nails are swept, materials are stacked, the yard looks like a work zone — not a disaster. We use magnetic rollers to catch nails in the grass and driveway.
