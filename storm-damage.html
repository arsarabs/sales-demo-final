03 / Common questions

What homeowners ask before they call.

How do I know if I have hail damage?

You often can't tell from the ground. Common signs: dented gutters, AC unit fins that look like they took BBs, bruised shingles (look dark and soft when pressed), and cracked ridge cap. Call us — we'll come look for free.

What if my claim gets denied?

We've successfully appealed denials before. We can request a re-inspection with our documentation and, if needed, recommend a public adjuster to fight it. Denial isn't always final.

How long does the claim process take?

Typically 2–6 weeks from inspection to approved claim. Install usually follows within 1–2 weeks of approval. During peak hail season (May–August), we schedule as fast as material delivery allows.

Can I pick my own shingles?

Yes. Your insurance pays for like-for-like replacement, but you can upgrade the shingle line and pay the difference. Danny will walk you through color and product options before signing anything.

Do I need to be home for the inspection?

Not for the initial roof inspection — we just need access to the exterior. You should plan to be home for the adjuster meeting, which usually runs 45–60 minutes.

What does the inspection actually cover?

Full roof surface, all penetrations (vents, skylights, pipes), gutters, downspouts, flashing, chimney cap, and ridge cap. We photograph everything and give you a written summary.
